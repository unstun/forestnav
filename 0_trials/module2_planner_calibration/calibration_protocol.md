all three run in a single `run_main_evaluation` invocation per query-seed set:

- M2 `ha_dang_multi_rs` — Dang multi-curvature RS.
- M3 `ha_rl_rs_ppo` — HA* + RL-RS funnel operator, deterministic policy,
  frozen Gate3-failed checkpoint (below).

## Pre-Registered Analysis (computed after all three sets finish)

Per set x bucket (Complex, Extreme) x method: success rate, timeout rate,
median/p95 expansions, median/p95 end-to-end time, collision violations
(must be 0), M3 operator telemetry (rl_attempts, rl_successes, mean
nn_forward_time_s, fallbacks). Paired per-query M3 vs M1 median expansions
ratio and median time ratio. Path-length comparison M3 vs M1 on jointly
successful queries (the standard runner has no vanilla reference method, so
inflation is computed in the aggregation step from paired path lengths).

Across the three sets, for each headline quantity report min / median / max —
that spread is the calibration deliverable. Explicitly reported quantities:

1. Complex success delta (M3 - M1), pp.
2. Complex paired median time ratio (M3/M1).
3. Complex paired median expansions ratio (M3/M1).
4. Extreme success delta (M3 - M1), pp.
5. Extreme paired median time ratio (M3/M1).
6. Easy success delta (M3 - M1), pp (sanity check only).

No thresholds, no bands, no verdict here: threshold-setting happens in the
lane-C contract draft, which Dr Sun reviews and approves separately.

  stop and report instead of shrinking the protocol. Two consecutive
